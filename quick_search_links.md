## How to Use These Links

1. **Click each link** - Opens CourtListener search results
2. **Review case list** - Look for case names with major semiconductor companies
3. **Click into cases** - View full docket sheet
4. **Search docket for**: "fees", "costs", "lodestar", "expert witness"
5. **Download PDFs** - Fee petition documents usually 20-50 pages
6. **Look for exhibits** - Billing rate tables, expert declarations

---

## What to Look for in Docket Entries

### Key Document Titles:
- "Motion for Attorney Fees and Costs"
- "Memorandum in Support of Fee Application"
- "Declaration of [Attorney Name] in Support of Fee Petition"
- "Exhibit A - Lodestar Calculation"
- "Expert Witness Fee Statement"
- "Bill of Costs"
- "Prevailing Party Fee Motion"

### Typical Document Numbers:
- Fee petitions usually filed as ECF/Docket Entry #100-200 (after trial/summary judgment)
- Look for entries 30-90 days after "Final Judgment" or "Order Granting Summary Judgment"

## Troubleshooting

**If no results appear:**
1. Try removing some search terms (less restrictive)
2. Search for company names only, then add "fees" filter
3. Try date range: 2023-2024 only (most recent)
4. Remove court filter to see all districts

**If too many results:**
1. Add lodestar requirement
2. Narrow to single district court
3. Add specific technology term (DRAM, GPU, etc.)
4. Filter by specific companies
