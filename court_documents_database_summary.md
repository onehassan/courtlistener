## METHODOLOGY

**Extractability Assessment:**
- ✅ **High**: Structured data, specific amounts regularly visible in judgments
- ⚠️ **Medium**: Cases contain fee data but require manual review/parsing
- ❌ **Low**: Fee data rarely present or not systematically available
- 📊 **Varies**: Depends on case type/court level
