## EASTERN EUROPE

### POLAND

#### **93. European e-Justice Portal - Poland**
- **URL**: https://e-justice.europa.eu/37/EN/costs?POLAND
- **Content**: Court fees and costs information for Poland
- **Format**: Online information portal

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Relative Fee**: 5% of dispute value (min PLN 30, max PLN 100,000)
- **Fixed Fee**: PLN 600 (~€140) for divorce, personal rights protection, copyright
- **Enforcement Fee**: 5-10% of enforced amount (max PLN 50,000 / ~€11,000)

**Database Access:**
- No central public register of all court judgments
- Some judgments published in online databases (anonymized)
- Access via public information requests

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **25%** - No centralized database, limited online access
**Note:** Judgments available through access to public information provisions

### CZECH REPUBLIC

#### **94. European e-Justice Portal - Czech Republic**
- **URL**: https://e-justice.europa.eu/37/EN/costs?CZECH_REPUBLIC
- **Authority**: Ministry of Justice
- **Content**: Court fees and costs framework

**Legal Framework:**
- **Decree No. 177/1996**: Lawyers' tariff for legal services fees
- **Act No. 549/1991**: Court fees in civil proceedings
- **2013 Reform**: Decree No. 484/2000 abolished; attorney fees now under tariff only

**Fee Structure:**
- **"Loser Pays" System**: Unsuccessful party pays costs (with exceptions)
- **Appeals**: 50% of fee paid at lower court

**Database Access:**
- Collection of Laws published by Ministry of Internal Affairs
- Free public inspection
- No specific centralized case law database identified

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **20%** - Legal framework available, limited case law database
**Note:** Legislation accessible; court decisions require direct court contact

### ROMANIA

#### **95. European e-Justice Portal - Romania**
- **URL**: https://e-justice.europa.eu/37/EN/costs?ROMANIA
- **Portal**: Courts' Portal (Ministry of Justice)
- **Content**: Court judgments, summaries

**Court Fees:**
- **Stamp Fees**: 8% for values up to RON 500 (min RON 20)
- **Lawyers' Fees**: Variable, based on difficulty, size, duration
- **Fee List**: Established by Ministry of Justice + National Union of Judicial Executors

**Database Access:**
- High Court of Cassation and Justice publishes case law on website
- Courts' Portal (Ministry of Justice) has summaries
- Information not easily available on public websites

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **30%** - Limited online accessibility
**Official Publisher:** High Court website + Courts' Portal

## AFRICA & MIDDLE EAST

### MOROCCO

#### **113. Judicial Portal of Morocco**
- **URL**: https://juriscassation.cspj.ma/en
- **Launch**: 26 January 2022
- **Content**: Court of Cassation jurisprudence + ordinary and specialized courts of appeals/first instance
- **Access**: FREE digital platform

**Court Fees:**
- **General Rule**: 1% of amount in dispute (taxe judiciaire)
- **Free Cases**: Labor law, family law
- **Additional Fees**: Procedural acts (e.g., lawsuit service), expert opinions

**Cost Allocation:**
- **General Rule**: Losing party bears all court fees
- **Attorney Fees**: Each party bears own (regardless of outcome)

**Tribunal Types:**
- Tribunal de Première Instance (Court of First Instance)
- Tribunal de Commerce (Commercial Court)
- Tribunal Administratif (Administrative Court)

**Extractability Score:** ✅ **60%** - Free official platform launched 2022
**Note:** New digital platform improving access

### EGYPT

#### **114. Egyptian Court Databases**
- **Tashreeat Database**: Soft copies of rulings (CDs, USBs, database)
- **Supreme Constitutional Court Website**: Available in Arabic, English, French
- **Collection of Awards**: Manual searches still important

**Court-Imposed Costs:**
- **Attorney Fees Award**: EGP 50-200 (depending on judicial degree)
- **Contingency Fees**: Up to 20% of successful outcome (Law No. 17/1983)
- **Enforcement Costs**: 7.5% on commencement + 2.5% per attempt

**Recent Fee Increases:**
- Photocopying: EGP 1 → EGP 5+ per page
- Certificate of non-appeal: EGP 25 → EGP 60.5
- Some fees increased 300-900%

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **35%** - Databases not entirely complete
**Note:** Tashreeat available but incomplete; manual searches still needed

**Professional Expenses:**
- Generally 5-10% of lawyer's fees as lump sum
