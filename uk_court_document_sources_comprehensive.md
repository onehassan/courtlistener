## FREE OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT DATABASES

### **1. Find Case Law (The National Archives)**
- **URL**: https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/
- **Launch**: 2022
- **Authority**: The National Archives
- **Content**: Judgments and decisions from UK courts and tribunals
- **Coverage**: 2003 onwards (superior courts)
- **Format**: HTML, searchable full text
- **Access**: **FREE**

**Courts Covered:**
- Supreme Court
- Court of Appeal (Civil & Criminal)
- High Court (all divisions)
- Court of Protection
- Employment Appeal Tribunal
- Upper Tribunal
- Many specialist tribunals

**Financial Data:** Search for "costs" judgments
**Extractability Score:** ✅ **75%** - Excellent for costs cases
**Note:** Official UK government database, most authoritative source

---

### **2. UK Supreme Court Website**
- **URL**: https://supremecourt.uk/
- **Latest Judgments**: https://supremecourt.uk/news/latest-judgments.html
- **Content**: All Supreme Court decisions
- **Format**: PDF and HTML
- **Access**: **FREE**

**Court Fees (2024):**
- **Permission to Appeal**: £1,390 (civil) - increased from £1,000 in April 2024
- **First increase since 2011**
- **Future updates**: Every 2 years by inflation

**Help with Fees:** Guidance published December 2, 2024
**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **45%** - Costs discussed in many judgments
**Note:** Fee increases came into force April 2024

## TRIBUNALS - EMPLOYMENT & IMMIGRATION

### **15. Employment Tribunal**
- **Decisions**: Published via GOV.UK and BAILII
- **Database**: New system between March-May 2021
- **Statistics**: Ministry of Justice quarterly and annual reports

**Key Data (2023/24):**
- **Total Claims**: 97,000 (up from 86,000 - 13% increase)
- **Average Unfair Dismissal Award**: £13,749 (up 15% from £11,914)
- **Highest Unfair Dismissal**: £179,124
- **Highest Overall Award**: £995,128 (sex discrimination)

**Compensation Caps (April 2024):**
- **Basic Award**: £17,130 cap
- **Compensatory Award**: £105,707 or 52 weeks' gross pay (whichever lower)
- **No Cap**: Whistleblowing, health & safety dismissals, discrimination claims

**Tribunal Fees:**
- **ABOLISHED July 2017** (Supreme Court ruled unlawful)
- **Proposed reintroduction**: November 2024, generating £0.6-0.7M to March 2025, £1.3-1.7M/year thereafter

**Costs Orders:**
- **Exception, not the rule**
- **Preparation Time Order**: £44/hour for unrepresented winning parties (April 2024-April 2025)

**Extractability Score:** ✅ **70%** - Award statistics published regularly
**Note:** FREE access, no filing fees (currently)

## TRIBUNALS - TAX, PROPERTY & SPECIALIZED

### **17. Tax Tribunal**
- **URL**: https://www.gov.uk/tax-tribunal-decisions
- **Alternative**: https://taxandchancery_ut.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk/
- **Find Case Law**: May 2022+ decisions
- **Access**: **FREE**

**Costs Structure:**

**First-tier Tribunal (FTT):**
- **NO tribunal fees** to issue tax appeal
- **General Rule**: Each party bears own costs
- **Exception - Complex Cases**: Costs may be awarded
- **Wasted Costs Orders**: Available for unreasonable conduct

**Upper Tribunal:**
- **Standard Position**: Unsuccessful party pays legal costs
- **No opt-out** from costs regime

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **35%** - Costs awards limited to complex cases
**Note:** Different costs regime than most tribunals

---

### **18. Property Tribunal / Lands Chamber**
- **First-tier**: Residential Property Chamber
- **Upper Tribunal**: Lands Chamber
- **URL**: https://landschamber.decisions.tribunals.gov.uk/
- **Access**: **FREE**

**Costs Rules:**

**First-tier Tribunal (Residential Property):**
- Usually each party bears own costs
- Tribunal fees can be recovered
- Loss of earnings, travel, photocopying harder to prove

**Upper Tribunal (Lands Chamber):**
- **General Rule**: Each party bears own costs unless ordered
- **Detailed Assessment Fee**: 5% of costs assessed
- **Recoverable**: Assessment fee can be recovered as part of assessed costs

**First Costs Decision:** Completed in CAAD appeals (Compulsory Acquisition and Compensation Appeals Division)

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **40%** - Some costs decisions available
**Note:** Different rules for residential vs. commercial property

### **20. Court of Protection**
- **URL**: Decisions on Find Case Law and BAILII
- **Costs Section on BAILII**: https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/Costs/

**Application Fees (May 2025):**
- **Initial Application**: £421
- **Appeals**: £265
- **Hearing Fee**: £495 (some cases)

**Fee Assistance:**
- Form COP44A for fee exemption
- Based on financial circumstances
- Legal aid available (deprivation of liberty challenges - no means test)

**Costs Structure:**
- **Property/Financial Affairs**: Paid from 'P's money
- **Health/Welfare**: Each party bears own costs

**Example Cases:**
- PLK & Ors [2020] EWHC B28 (Costs): Detailed rates £135-£690/hour

**Extractability Score:** ✅ **80%** - Excellent costs data in SCCO cases
**Note:** Mental Capacity Act 2005 governs Court of Protection

### **21. Family Court**
- **Transparency Initiative**: 2024
- **Practice Guidance**: June 2024
- **Publications**: Pilot scheme extended November 2024

**Judgment Publication:**
- Every legal adviser/judge should publish 5-10 judgments/year
- Anonymisation required (major bottleneck)
- Transparency Implementation Group (TIG) established

**Pilot Locations (2024):**
- Royal Courts of Justice (from November 11, 2024)
- Central Family Court, Birmingham, Leeds (from January 29, 2024)
- All 19 pilot areas - public/private law (from November 4, 2024)

**Challenges:**
- Impractical for most judges to anonymize 10% of cases
- Only High Court judges have administrative help

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **30%** - Limited published decisions
**Note:** Major transparency reforms in progress 2024
