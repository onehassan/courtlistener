## How to Access Fee Documents

### Step 1: Visit CourtListener Docket Page
Click on any `courtlistener_url` from the JSON file to view the complete docket sheet.

Example: https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/69875031/

### Step 2: Search for Fee-Related Docket Entries
Look for entries containing:
- "Motion for Attorney Fees"
- "Petition for Attorney Fees"
- "Bill of Costs"
- "Fee Application"
- "35 USC 285" (exceptional case statute)
- "ORDER" + "attorney fees"
- "Taxation of Costs"

### Step 3: Download PDFs
- Click "Download PDF" links next to relevant docket entries
- RECAP documents are often freely available
- Some recent documents may require PACER access

### Step 4: Extract Fee Data
Patent fee documents typically contain:
- **Hourly Rates:** Partner, associate, paralegal rates
- **Total Hours:** By timekeeper and task
- **Total Fees:** Requested and/or awarded
- **Basis for Fees:** Exceptional case, prevailing party, etc.
- **Lodestar Calculation:** Base hours × rates
- **Multipliers:** If any enhancement applied
- **Court Analysis:** Reasonableness determination

## Patent Fee Award Standards

### 35 USC § 285 - Exceptional Cases
The patent statute allows courts to award attorney fees to the prevailing party in **exceptional cases**. Cases are exceptional when they involve:
- Litigation misconduct
- Frivolous infringement claims
- Frivolous invalidity defenses
- Bad faith conduct
- Inequitable conduct

### Typical Patent Fee Ranges (Based on Industry Data)
- **Patent Partners:** $600-1,200/hour
- **Senior Associates:** $400-700/hour
- **Junior Associates:** $250-500/hour
- **Patent Agents:** $200-400/hour
- **Paralegals:** $100-250/hour
- **Technical Experts:** $300-800/hour

*Note: Rates vary significantly by geography, firm size, and case complexity*
