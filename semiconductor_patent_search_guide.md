## Advanced Search Strategy

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Initial Broad Search**
   - Use Search 1 to identify recent semiconductor patent cases
   - Look for cases with multiple docket entries (indicates active litigation)
   - Note case numbers and party names

2. **Filter by Technology Type**
   - Use Searches 2-4 based on specific technology area
   - Cross-reference with company names (foundries, fabless designers)

3. **Identify Fee Petition Documents**
   - Once you find a case, click into the docket
   - Search docket entries for:
     - "Motion for Attorney Fees"
     - "Fee Petition"
     - "Bill of Costs"
     - "Expert Witness Declaration"
     - Numbers like "ECF No. [X]" where X is document number

4. **Locate Lodestar Analysis**
   - Download fee petition documents (usually PDF)
   - Search for keywords:
     - "lodestar"
     - "hourly rate"
     - "hours expended"
     - "multiplier"
     - "billing rates"
     - "expert witness fees"

5. **Verify Expert Witness Billing**
   - Look for exhibits to fee petitions
   - Check for:
     - Technical consultant timesheets
     - Expert witness declarations with hourly rates
     - Breakdown of expert costs vs. attorney fees

## Example Case Structure to Look For

**Ideal Case Profile:**
- **Case Name**: [Major Semiconductor Co.] v. [Competitor]
- **Court**: D. Delaware / N.D. California / W.D. Texas
- **Filed**: 2022-2025
- **Technology**: DRAM patents, GPU architecture, lithography process
- **Docket Entry Example**: "ECF No. 145 - Plaintiff's Motion for Attorney Fees and Costs"
- **Document Contains**:
  - Lodestar calculation table
  - Attorney hourly rates ($500-$1,200/hr)
  - Expert witness rates ($400-$800/hr)
  - Technical consultant billing ($300-$600/hr)
  - Total hours breakdown by task

## Tips for Efficient Searching

1. **Use Date Filters**: Filed after 01/01/2022, filed before 12/31/2025
2. **Sort by Relevance**: order_by=score desc (default)
3. **Check Docket Length**: Cases with 100+ docket entries likely have fee petitions
4. **Look for "Prevailing Party"**: Fee petitions typically filed after judgment
5. **Check Related Cases**: Semiconductor companies often have multiple related suits
6. **Download PDFs**: Fee petitions are usually in PDF format, search within document
7. **Review Exhibits**: Billing details often in exhibits rather than main motion

---

## Alternative Search Approach

If the above searches don't yield results, try:

### Reverse Search Strategy
1. Search for known semiconductor cases (e.g., Samsung v. SK Hynix, NVIDIA v. Samsung)
2. Check their dockets for fee petitions
3. Use similar case citations to find related matters

### PACER Direct Search
- CourtListener pulls from PACER
- If cases are very recent (last 3-6 months), may need direct PACER access
- CourtListener may have indexing delay
