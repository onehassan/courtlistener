## ⚠️ **Challenges Finding Agtech Cases in Target Range**

### Why Limited Results:

1. **Fewer Cases Overall**
   - Agricultural patent litigation less common than tech/semiconductor
   - Many disputes settle pre-litigation

2. **Geographic Concentration**
   - Cases concentrate in Delaware (major patent venue)
   - N.D. Iowa has limited federal patent dockets
   - C.D. Illinois has some cases but fewer than expected

3. **Technology Gap**
   - Drone/sensor technology too new for extensive litigation
   - Autonomous equipment still in development
   - Many precision ag companies license rather than litigate

4. **Trade Secrets vs. Patents**
   - Much agricultural innovation protected as trade secrets
   - Fewer public patent cases as result

5. **Industry Consolidation**
   - Major companies (Deere, AGCO, CNH) often cross-license
   - Reduces litigation between major players
