## JAPAN

### ⚠️ **Japan - Supreme Court**

#### **Court Costs Structure**
- **Court Filing Fees** (borne by losing party):
  - ¥10 million claim: **¥50,000 filing fee**
  - ¥30 million claim: **¥110,000 filing fee**
  - ¥100 million claim: **¥320,000 filing fee**
  - ¥1 billion claim: **¥3,000,000 filing fee**

**Attorney Fees:**
- **General Rule**: Each party pays own attorney fees (not recoverable)
- **Exception**: Tort claims - court may award approximately **10% of granted amount**

**Shimonoseki Trial (Supreme Court, 25 March 2003):**
- Award: **¥300,000 per plaintiff**
- Source: Wikipedia

**Note:** Japan does not follow "loser pays" rule for attorney fees. Court costs (filing fees, witness expenses) are recoverable but not attorney fees except 10% in tort cases.

## KEY FINDINGS

### ✅ **BEST SOURCES WITH READILY VISIBLE HOURLY RATES:**

1. **UK - BAILII**: Excellent. Multiple detailed assessment judgments with specific hourly rates clearly stated in pounds sterling across multiple grades and experience levels.

2. **UK - Judiciary Official Guidelines**: Excellent. Published official guideline hourly rates table with specific amounts for all grades and geographic bands.

3. **South Africa - SAFLII**: Very Good. Court judgments clearly state hourly and daily rates for counsel and attorneys in Rands.

4. **Kenya - Kenya Law**: Very Good. Numerous recent cases with specific taxed costs amounts in Kenya Shillings, though typically total bills rather than hourly rates.

5. **Singapore - ELITIGATION**: Good. Some cases discuss hourly rates, particularly for taxation matters and litigants in person.

6. **Ghana - GBA**: Good. Official fee schedules with specific amounts, though typically per-case fees rather than hourly rates.

7. **India - IndianKanoon**: Good for fee structure information. Per-appearance fees rather than hourly rates. Law Commission reports provide valuable context.

### ⚠️ **SOURCES WITH LIMITED VISIBILITY:**

8. **Hong Kong**: Rates exist but full tables not freely downloadable online.

9. **Malaysia**: Judgments available but specific cost awards require manual case-by-case review.

10. **Norway**: Court judgments primarily in Norwegian; market rates available but court-awarded rates not easily accessible in English.

11. **Switzerland**: Federal Supreme Court Dataset available for bulk analysis but individual HTML cases don't prominently display rates; market rates available.

12. **Japan**: Court filing fees clearly specified; attorney fees generally not awarded except 10% in tort cases.

13. **Iceland**: Full judgments in Icelandic only; limited English accessibility.

## CONCLUSION

**Highest Quality Sources for Downloadable Court Documents with Visible Financial Data:**

1. **United Kingdom (BAILII)** - Multiple cases with detailed hourly rate breakdowns
2. **United Kingdom (Official Guidelines)** - Published guideline rate tables
3. **South Africa (SAFLII)** - Court judgments with specific hourly/daily rates
4. **Kenya (Kenya Law)** - Recent cases with specific taxed costs amounts
5. **Singapore (ELITIGATION)** - Some cases with hourly rate discussions

**Regions Requiring Additional Research:**
- Hong Kong, Malaysia, Norway, Switzerland, Iceland require direct database access or language expertise
- Japan and India use different fee structures (per-appearance, filing fees) rather than hourly billing for cost awards
