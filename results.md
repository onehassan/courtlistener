## Case 5: Cortorreal v. Reyes Fastest Shipping Inc.

**Case Information:**
- **Case Name:** Mercy Cortorreal v. Reyes Fastest Shipping Inc., et al.
- **Docket Number:** 1:24-cv-03948-HJR
- **Court:** Southern District of New York (SDNY)
- **Date Filed/Decided:** February 20, 2025
- **Document Type:** Order (Reviewing Proposed Settlement - NOT YET APPROVED)
- **Practice Area:** Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) - Wage and Hour Violations
- **Fee Statute:** 29 U.S.C. § 201 et seq. (FLSA)
- **Prevailing Party:** Plaintiff (proposed settlement)
- **Law Firm:** [Not named in order]
- **Attorney:** Jacob Aronauer

**Timekeepers:**

| Name | Role | Proposed Hourly Rate | Court's Reasonable Rate† | Hours | Lodestar (Proposed) | Lodestar (Court Rate) |
|------|------|---------------------|-------------------------|-------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| Jacob Aronauer | Attorney/Partner | $500 | $400 | 10.7 | $5,350 | $4,280 |
| Paralegals (unnamed) | Paralegals | $175 | $125 | 12.84 | $2,247 | $1,605 |

**†** Court stated these lower rates are "well within the range that courts in this district regularly approve as reasonable"

**Totals:**
- **Total Settlement Amount:** $8,000
- **Attorney's Fees and Costs Requested:** $2,936.68 (33.33% of settlement after costs)
  - Costs: $405
  - Attorney's Fees (net of costs): $2,531.68
- **Plaintiff's Recovery:** $5,063.32 (63.29% of settlement)
- **Total Hours:** 23.54 hours (10.7 attorney + 12.84 paralegal)
- **Lodestar (at proposed rates):** $7,597
- **Lodestar (at court's reasonable rates):** $5,885
- **Lodestar Multiplier:** 0.33 (at proposed rates), 0.43 (at court's reasonable rates)
- **Blended Rate (proposed):** $323/hour ($7,597 / 23.54 hours)
- **Blended Rate (court rates):** $250/hour ($5,885 / 23.54 hours)

**Plaintiff's Potential Recovery (if successful at trial):**
- Unpaid minimum wages: $5,800
- Unpaid overtime wages: $5,220
- Liquidated damages: $11,020
- Wage notice damages: $10,000
- **Total Potential:** $32,040
- **Settlement as % of potential:** 25%

**Key Notes:**
- **Settlement Status:** NOT YET APPROVED - Court rejected due to "Covenant Not to Sue" provision
- **Court's Findings:**
  - Overall settlement amount ($8,000) is **fair and reasonable**
  - Attorney's fees ($2,936.68) are **reasonable**
  - "Covenant Not to Sue" provision preventing plaintiff from assisting other claimants is contrary to FLSA remedial aims
- **Fee Structure:** Contingency fee at 33.33% after reimbursement of costs
- **Lodestar Multiplier Context:** Court noted that "multiplier near 2 should, in most cases, be sufficient compensation for the risk associated with contingent fees in FLSA cases" (citing Fujiwara v. Sushi Yasuda Ltd., 58 F. Supp. 3d 424, 439 (S.D.N.Y. 2014))
- **Very Low Multiplier:** This case had multiplier of only 0.33-0.43, meaning fees were well below lodestar
- **Court Approved Rate Benchmarks for SDNY:**
  - Partners: $400/hour is reasonable (citing Rodriguez v. 3551 Realty Co., 2017 WL 5054728; Montes v. 11 Hanover Grp. LLC, 2019 WL 4392516)
  - Paralegals: $100-$150/hour is typical (citing Diaz-Caballero v. Midtown Catch Corp., 2020 WL 8880944)
- **Percentage Fee:** Courts in SDNY "routinely award one third of a settlement fund as a reasonable fee in FLSA cases" (citing Lazo v. Kim's Nails at York Ave., Inc., 2019 WL 95638)
- **Lodestar Cross-Check:** "Courts in this circuit use the lodestar method as a cross-check to ensure the reasonableness of attorneys' fees"
- **Case Complexity:** Relatively straightforward FLSA case with significant litigation risk (defendant's position was plaintiff entitled to nothing)
- **Settlement Discount Justified By:** Uncertainty of protracted litigation, motion practice, appeals, and difficulty collecting larger judgment

**Court's Fee Analysis Method:**
1. Percentage-of-settlement method: 33.33% after costs = $2,531.68 in fees
2. Lodestar cross-check:
   - At proposed rates: $7,597 lodestar vs. $2,531.68 fees = 0.33 multiplier
   - At court's reasonable rates: $5,885 lodestar vs. $2,531.68 fees = 0.43 multiplier
3. Conclusion: Fees reasonable because even at lower rates, multiplier is far below the typical 2.0 multiplier for FLSA contingency cases
