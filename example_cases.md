## How to Find Fee Petitions in These Cases

1. **Search for the case** using the queries above
2. **Click into the case** to view the docket
3. **Use browser search (Ctrl+F)** to find:
   - "fees"
   - "costs"
   - "285" (35 U.S.C. § 285 - attorney fees in patent cases)
   - "lodestar"
4. **Look for document types:**
   - Motions (filed by parties)
   - Declarations (supporting motions)
   - Orders (court rulings on fee motions)
5. **Download PDFs** of promising documents
6. **Search within PDFs** for:
   - Hourly rate tables
   - Expert witness declarations
   - Lodestar calculations
   - Billing summaries

## Expected Timeline for Fee Petitions

**Typical Case Timeline:**
- Filing: 2022-2023
- Summary Judgment or Trial: 2023-2024
- Fee Petition Filed: 30-60 days after judgment (2024-2025)
- Fee Decision: 3-6 months later (2024-2025)

**Where to Look:**
- Cases filed 2022: Check for fee petitions in 2023-2024
- Cases filed 2023: Check for fee petitions in 2024-2025
- Older cases (2019-2021): May have fee petitions in 2022-2023

## Red Flags for Good Cases

**Indicators a case likely has detailed fee petitions:**

1. **High damages award** ($10M+) = likely fee petition
2. **NPE plaintiff** = often triggers § 285 exceptional case fees
3. **Summary judgment for defendant** = defendant likely seeks fees
4. **"Exceptional case" mentioned** in orders = fees likely
5. **Long docket** (100+ entries) = complex case, more billing
6. **Multiple expert witnesses** mentioned = higher expert costs
7. **ITC parallel proceedings** = increased costs, more experts
