## **INTERNATIONAL TRIBUNALS & ARBITRATION**

### **Investment Arbitration**
81. ICSID (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes) - https://icsid.worldbank.org/
    - Administrative charge: US$52,000
    - Arbitrator fees: US$3,000/day
    - Average claimant costs (2011-2015): US$5,619,261.74

82. PCA (Permanent Court of Arbitration) - https://pca-cpa.org/
    - Tribunal/Institutional costs: 10-15% of total
    - Parties' own costs: 85-90% of total

### **Trade Disputes**
83. WTO Dispute Settlement - https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/dispu_e.htm
    - No reimbursement for legal expenses to winning party
    - One-page case summaries: 211 cases (1995-2022)

### **International Arbitration Institutions**
84. ICC (International Chamber of Commerce) - https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/
    - Party costs: 83% of total (lawyers' fees)
    - ICC Commission Report downloadable
    - Costs calculator available

85. LCIA (London Court of International Arbitration) - https://www.lcia.org/
    - Median cost: USD 117,653
    - Median duration: 20 months
    - 2024 Report: 616 cases analyzed

86. SIAC (Singapore International Arbitration Centre) - https://siac.org.sg/
    - Median cost: USD 29,567
    - Median duration: 11.7 months
    - Filing fees: S$3,000-3,270

87. HKIAC (Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre) - https://www.hkiac.org/
    - Max arbitrator rate: HKD 6,500/hour
    - Emergency arbitrator: HKD 250,000 deposit
    - Multiple fee schedules (2008-2024)
