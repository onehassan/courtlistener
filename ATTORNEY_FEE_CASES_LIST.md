## How to Access Fee Documents

### Step 1: Visit the Docket Page
Click on any `courtlistener_url` above to view the complete docket sheet.

### Step 2: Look for Fee-Related Entries
Search for docket entries containing:
- "Motion for Attorney Fees"
- "Bill of Costs"
- "Fee Petition"
- "ORDER granting" + "fees"
- "Taxation of Costs"

### Step 3: Download PDFs
- Click "Download PDF" links next to relevant docket entries
- RECAP documents are often freely available
- Some documents may require PACER access

### Step 4: Extract Fee Data
Look for:
- Hourly rates by attorney grade
- Total hours worked
- Total fees awarded
- Costs and disbursements
- Court's reasoning for fee determination
