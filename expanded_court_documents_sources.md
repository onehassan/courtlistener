## CONTINENTAL EUROPE - MAJOR JURISDICTIONS

### GERMANY

#### **68. Rechtsprechung im Internet**
- **URL**: https://www.rechtsprechung-im-internet.de/
- **Authority**: Federal Ministry of Justice and Federal Office of Justice
- **Content**: Selected decisions from Federal Constitutional Court, highest federal courts, Federal Patent Court
- **Coverage**: 2010 onwards
- **Format**: HTML, PDF, XML (all formats free)
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Features**: Anonymized, full text, case numbers, decision dates, legal principles

**Financial Data Available:**
- Court costs (Gerichtskosten)
- Attorney fees governed by Rechtsanwaltsvergütungsgesetz (RVG)
- Specific fee schedules downloadable

#### **69. REWIS Database**
- **URL**: https://rewis.io/urteile/
- **Content**: Free index of 240,000+ judgments in full text
- **Update**: Daily
- **Features**: Intelligently linked decisions

#### **70. Open Legal Data**
- **URL**: https://de.openlegaldata.io/
- **Type**: Non-profit project
- **Content**: Case law and legal norms
- **Features**: APIs, published datasets for processing and analysis
- **Format**: Technical tools for legal data processing

**German Legal Costs Framework:**
- **Rechtsanwaltsvergütungsgesetz (RVG)** - Attorneys' Remuneration Act
- **Gerichtskostengesetz (GKG)** - Court Costs Act
- **Online Fee Calculators**: Available at rarw.de and debtcollectionagency.de
- **Loser Pays Principle**: Defeated party bears costs

**Subscription Databases (Commercial):**
- **Juris** - Comprehensive, historical coverage from late 1940s
- **Beck-Online** - Extensive case law database

### FRANCE

#### **71. Legifrance**
- **URL**: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/
- **Authority**: Official French legal database
- **Content**: Legislation, regulations, case law
- **Note**: Court judgments available but no centralized costs database

**Financial Data:**
- **Court Filing Fees**: None for civil courts of first instance or labor council
- **Fixed Procedural Charges**:
  - Court of Appeal (criminal): €338
  - Court of Cassation: €422
- **Avocat (Attorney) Fees**:
  - Paris typical hourly rates: **€250-350** (plus VAT)
  - Freely determined by lawyers, no caps
- **Article 700 CPC**: Court can order one party to pay lump sum to other for legal costs
- **General Rule**: Each party pays own legal fees unless bad faith/temerity

**Cost Structure:**
- Filing costs (dépens) recoverable from losing party
- Legal fees (honoraires) generally not recoverable except via Article 700

### NETHERLANDS

#### **72. Rechtspraak.nl (Dutch Judiciary)**
- **URL**: https://www.rechtspraak.nl/
- **English Section**: https://www.rechtspraak.nl/English/
- **Content**: Official Dutch judiciary website with court information

**Netherlands Commercial Court (NCC):**
- **Costs Page**: https://www.rechtspraak.nl/English/NCC/Pages/costs.aspx
- **Format**: Tables of court fees and lawyers' fees
- **Downloadable**: PDF document on NCC and other European commercial courts

**Specific Fees (2024):**
- **Court Fees for Claims >€100,000**: €6,617
- **NCC Flat Fee**: €9,000 - €24,000
- **Maximum Rate**: Reached at €100,000 dispute value

**Cost Recovery:**
- **General Rule**: Article 237(1) CPC - "loser pays all"
- **Practical Reality**: Court-ordered costs usually fraction of actual lawyer fees
- **Lawyer Fees**: Hourly basis, varies by experience and complexity
- **Note**: Parties bear own costs initially; reimbursement after judgment

### AUSTRIA

#### **73. RIS - Rechtsinformationssystem (Legal Information System)**
- **URL**: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/
- **Authority**: Austrian Federal Chancellery
- **Content**: Judgments from all Austrian courts
- **Coverage**: 1980 onwards (all decisions available free)
- **Format**: XML, RTF, PDF, HTML (all downloadable)
- **Search**: By terms, paragraphs, other criteria

**Available Legal Texts:**
- **Gerichtsgebührengesetz (GGG)** - Court Fees Act
- **Gebührenanspruchsgesetz (GebAG)** - Fees Entitlement Act
- **Rechtsanwaltsordnung (RAO)** - Lawyers' Code
- **Rechtsanwaltstarifgesetz (RATG)** - Scales of Legal Fees Act

**Financial Data:**
- **Loser-Pays Principle**: Victorious party recovers costs to extent of winning percentage
- **Calculation**: Based on statutory Lawyers Tariff (Rechtsanwaltstarifgesetz)
- **Court Fees (High Disputes >€350,000)**: 1.2% of amount in dispute + €6,964
- **Note**: Statutory compensation typically falls short of actual hourly fees

### THAILAND

#### **75. Thai Courts System**
- **Court Fee Structure Available** (from law firm guides)

**Specific Fees:**
- **Court Filing Fee (Monetary Claims)**: 2% of claim amount
  - Cap: 200,000 baht (claims under 50M baht)
  - Additional 0.1% for amounts over 50M baht
- **Non-Monetary Claims**: 200 baht per case

**Attorney Fees (Court-Awarded):**
- **First Instance**: Maximum 5% of claim value, minimum 3,000 baht
- **Appeal**: Maximum 3% of claim value
- **Supreme Court**: Maximum 3% of claim value
- **Note**: Thai courts rarely grant full legal fees; awards well below market rates

**Market Attorney Rates:**
- Some firms: 7,500 baht/hour
- Others: 4,000 baht/hour (junior), 5,500 baht/hour (senior)

### INDONESIA

#### **76. Supreme Court of Indonesia (MARI - Mahkamah Agung RI)**
- **Official Website**: Visit directly for judgment database
- **Supreme Court Regulation No. 03 of 2012**: Process fees specified

**Specific Process Fees:**
- **Cassation (Civil Cases)**: Rp 500,000
- **Judicial Review (Civil Cases)**: Rp 2,500,000
- **Cassation (Commercial Civil Cases)**: Rp 5,000,000
- **Judicial Review (Commercial Civil Cases)**: Rp 10,000,000

**Administrative Fees:**
- **Per Case**: ~5 million rupiah upfront
- **Additional Party**: ~1 million rupiah incremental
- **Includes**: Registration, summons, stamps, witness oaths, proceedings, redaction, notification

**Attorney Fees:**
- No fixed standard in Indonesia
- Honorarium determined by fair agreement between parties
- **Cost Allocation**: Generally losing party pays (per judges' decision)
- **Exception**: Some cases require each party bear own expenses

### BRAZIL

#### **77. Superior Tribunal de Justiça (STJ)**
- **URL**: https://international.stj.jus.br/en/
- **Content**: Superior Court of Justice (highest for non-constitutional matters)

#### **78. Supremo Tribunal Federal (STF)**
- **URL**: https://www.stf.jus.br/
- **Content**: Supreme Federal Court (constitutional matters)
- **Downloadable**: Lawyers' fees document available (PDF)

**Attorney Fees (Honorários Advocatícios):**
- **Civil Litigation**: Fixed at **10-20%** of amount awarded
- **Calculation Basis**: Amount in judgment, economic advantage, or total amount in dispute
- **Payment**: Losing party must pay winning party's attorney fees

**Court Costs (Custas):**
- Vary by state
- Tabelas de custas (cost tables) provide values and payment methods
- Available for state courts and Superior Tribunals (STJ, STF)

**Court Structure:**
- STJ: Non-constitutional civil and criminal matters, foreign judgment issues
- STF: Federal Constitution matters only

### ARGENTINA

#### **79. Corte Suprema de Justicia de la Nación (CSJN)**
- **Authority**: Supreme Court of Justice of Argentina
- **Judicial Information Center**: Receives and publishes decisions
- **Principle**: Transparency in judicial decisions

**Attorney Fees (Honorarios):**
- **Governed By**: Local laws per jurisdiction + National Law No. 27,423
- **Pecuniary Matters**: **12-33%** of amount in dispute
- **Factors**: Complexity, results obtained, quality of services
- **Appellate Services**: **30-35%** of first-instance fees

**Costs:**
- **General Rule**: Defeated party bears all costs
- **Includes**: Attorney fees
- **Interest**: Awarded on costs for full compensation

- **Main Litigation Costs**: (i) Attorney fees, (ii) Translation fees, (iii) Administrative costs

## INTERNATIONAL ARBITRATION INSTITUTIONS

### ICC - INTERNATIONAL CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

#### **84. ICC International Court of Arbitration**
- **URL**: https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/
- **Costs Page**: https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/dispute-resolution-services/arbitration/costs-and-payment/
- **Costs Calculator**: https://iccwbo.org/dispute-resolution/dispute-resolution-services/arbitration/costs-and-payment/costs-calculator/

**Downloadable Resources:**
- **ICC Commission Report**: "Decisions on Costs in International Arbitration" (2015)
  - URL: https://iccwbo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2015/12/Decisions-on-Costs-in-International-Arbitration.pdf
- **Arbitration Rules Appendix III**: Arbitration Costs and Fees (PDF)
- **Cost Study**: https://www.international-arbitration-attorney.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/ICC-Arbitration-Cost-and-Fees.pdf

**Cost Structure:**
- **Basis**: Ad valorem (based on monetary value of claims)
- **Predictability**: Can estimate costs once claim value known
- **Components**:
  - Arbitrators' fees and expenses
  - ICC administrative expenses
  - Tribunal-appointed experts' fees
  - Reasonable legal and other costs of parties

**Key Finding:**
- **Party Costs**: 83% of total costs (lawyers' fees and expenses)
- **Institutional/Tribunal Costs**: 17% of total
- **Awards**: Attorney fees regularly awarded in ICC arbitrations

## REGIONAL HUMAN RIGHTS COURTS

### EUROPEAN COURT OF HUMAN RIGHTS

#### **88. ECtHR - HUDOC Database**
- **URL**: https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/
- **Content**: Case law of the Court - Grand Chamber, Chamber, and Committee judgments/decisions
- **Coverage**: Communicated cases, advisory opinions, legal summaries
- **Access**: Freely available online
- **Features**: Tutorial videos available for search functions

**Just Satisfaction (Damages & Legal Costs):**
- **Awards**: Pecuniary or non-pecuniary damages called "just satisfaction"
- **Typical Amounts**: Rarely exceed £1,000 plus legal costs (small vs. national courts)
- **Payment**: Vast majority paid promptly by State within 3 months
- **Default Interest**: Applies if payment not made within specified period

**Costs for Lawyers:**
- Must explain estimated costs (lawyers' fees and expenses)
- Rules on just satisfaction awards
- Search HUDOC for specific cost awards in cases

## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY NOTES

### Search Strategies Used:
1. **Official court websites** + "judgments costs fees downloadable"
