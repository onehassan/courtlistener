## ⚠️ **Why Construction Materials Cases Are So Limited:**

### 1. **Different IP Protection Strategy**
- **Trade secrets** preferred over patents for:
  - Concrete admixture formulations
  - Manufacturing processes
  - Installation techniques
- **Result:** Fewer patent cases, more trade secret disputes

### 2. **Industry Consolidation**
- Major players (Owens Corning, CertainTeed, Johns Manville, GAF) often cross-license
- Reduces litigation between major manufacturers
- NPEs less active in construction materials

### 3. **Technology Characteristics**
- Many innovations are **incremental improvements**
- Harder to prove infringement (composition claims)
- Lower damages compared to tech/pharma
- Less incentive to litigate

### 4. **Geographic Factors**
- Construction litigation concentrated in **different venues**:
  - Product liability: Various state courts
  - Contract disputes: Local courts
  - Patent cases: Scattered across districts
- **Target districts see less activity:**
  - N.D. Illinois: Manufacturing hub but limited construction patent cases
  - S.D.N.Y.: Not a construction materials center
  - D. Delaware: Some cases but fewer than tech

### 5. **Market Dynamics**
- **Building codes** drive standardization
- **Commodity products** = lower profit margins
- **Regional distribution** = local disputes, not federal
- **Long product cycles** = slower patent litigation
