## SOUTH AMERICA - MISSING COUNTRIES

### URUGUAY

#### **136. Base de Jurisprudencia Nacional (BJN)**
- **URL**: http://bjn.poderjudicial.gub.uy/
- **Authority**: Poder Judicial de Uruguay
- **Content**: Supreme Court and higher court decisions
- **Coverage**: 1989 to present
- **Database Size**: 100,000+ sentences published online
- **Access**: **FREE** - No registration required

**Database Features:**
- All definitive and interlocutory sentences
- Simple and advanced search
- Full-text judgments
- Public access

#### **137. CADE Database**
- **URL**: https://www.cade.com.uy/
- **Content**: Jurisprudence, legislation, and legal doctrine
- **Format**: Commercial database (subscription)
- **Claim**: Most complete legal database in Uruguay

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **General Rule**: Each party bears own costs
- **Exception**: Court may order claimant to pay costs in specific cases
- **Contingency Arrangements**: Not specified in fee schedule
- **No Fixed Tariff**: Fees negotiated between attorney and client

**Cost Allocation:**
- Only court can order losing party to pay costs
- Counter-guarantees required for injunctive relief
- Security for damages

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **30%** - Limited systematic fee data in judgments
**Database Access:** Excellent (100K+ free cases)
**Note:** Strong database access but limited fee recovery system (each party pays own costs)

### PARAGUAY

#### **138. Corte Suprema de Justicia - Base de Jurisprudencia**
- **URL**: https://www.csj.gov.py/jurisprudencia/
- **Authority**: Instituto de Investigaciones Jurídicas (IIJ)
- **Content**: Supreme Court resolutions from all chambers
- **Coverage**: 1995 to present
- **Access**: **FREE** - No username or password required

**Database Features:**
- Simple and advanced search options
- Summarization and indexing methodology
- Related legislation and jurisprudence cross-references
- Constantly updated

#### **139. Resoluciones Web**
- **URL**: https://www.csj.gov.py/resolucionesweb/
- **Content**: Appeals Tribunal resolutions
- **Access**: **FREE**

#### **140. vLex Paraguay**
- **URL**: https://vlex.com.br/coverage/paraguay
- **Content**: Legislation, case law, administrative decisions
- **Format**: Commercial database (subscription)
- **Volume**: Thousands of legal documents

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Law No. 1376**: Arancel de Honorarios de Abogados y Procuradores
- **Contingency Fees (Cuota Litis)**: Permitted, maximum 45% of net result
- **Prohibition**: Cannot be used for family support or labor cases
- **Fee Regulation**: Judge regulates fees ex officio in final decision

**Collection Rights:**
- Professional can demand payment from losing party OR client
- Client may seek reimbursement from losing party
- Written fee agreements required

**Arbitration Exception:**
- Attorney fees only part of costs if parties agreed beforehand
- Supreme Court clarified fees not automatic in arbitration

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **35%** - Structured fee system, court regulates fees
**Database Access:** Excellent (1995+ free access)
**Note:** Strong legal framework for fee recovery with judicial regulation

#### **142. Tribunal Constitucional Plurinacional**
- **URL**: https://buscador.tcpbolivia.bo/
- **Content**: Constitutional court decisions
- **Search Tool**: "Tree of Jurisprudence" systematization

#### **143. vLex Bolivia**
- **URL**: https://bolivia.vlex.com/
- **Content**: Comprehensive legal documents
- **Format**: Commercial database (subscription)

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Article 274 CPC**: Losing party pays costs
- **Costas Procesales Include**: Court fees + judicial costs + attorney fees + auxiliary personnel
- **Minimum Fee Schedule (Aranceles)**: Established by Ministerial Resolution (MJTI-DGAJ-Z-143-2023)

**Practical Costs:**
- Precautionary process: 1,000-3,500 bolivianos
- Divorce: 3,000-7,500 bolivianos
- Criminal process: 8,000-15,000 USD
- Percentage-based system common: Defendant pays percentage as sanction

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **40%** - Losing party pays system with statutory minimums
**Database Access:** Excellent (official databases well-maintained)
**Note:** Clear "loser pays" system with government-established minimum fees

- **URL**: https://vlex.ec/
- **Content**: Supreme Court decisions and legal documents

#### **148. Lexis Ecuador**
- **URL**: https://www.lexis.com.ec/
- **Content**: 500,000+ documents (legislation, jurisprudence, doctrine)
- **Claim**: Most extensive legal database in Ecuador
- **Format**: Commercial database (subscription)

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **COGEP Article 285**: Costas = judicial expenses + attorney fees + expert fees + publication costs + copies/certifications
- **COGEP Article 287**: Costs awarded when party litigated recklessly or in bad faith
- **No Fee Schedule**: Fees depend on each case and legal area
- **Free Market**: Fees freely negotiated between lawyer and client

**Judicial Discretion:**
- Judge determines if litigation was abusive, malicious, or reckless
- No exemption for reckless litigation
- Includes attorney fees of affected party

**Practical Fee Ranges:**
- Child support: $300
- Ordinary civil proceedings: ~$2,000
- Constitutional matters: Up to $20,000

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **35%** - Bad faith/reckless litigation standard, judicial discretion
**Database Access:** Excellent (multiple free and commercial databases)
**Note:** No fixed tariff creates uncertainty; judge has wide discretion

#### **151. vLex Venezuela**
- **URL**: https://vlexvenezuela.com/
- **Content**: TSJ case law, recent 2025 rulings
- **Format**: Commercial database (subscription)

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Article 274 CPC**: Losing party sentenced to pay costs
- **Article 286 CPC**: Maximum limit 30% of claim value
- **Simple Calculation**: Claim value × 30%
- **No Fixed Tariff**: But constitutional maximum

**Gratuity Principle:**
- **Constitution Article 26 & 254**: Justice is free
- **Prohibition**: No court fees, tariffs, or payments for judicial services
- **Eliminated**: Stamped paper, stamps, fiscal stamps, judicial fees
- **Remain**: Fees for auxiliaries (associate judges, court-appointed experts)

**Retasa (Fee Challenge):**
- Right to challenge excessive fee estimates
- Must be exercised within 10 business days

**Current Market Rates:**
- Average: $20-100 USD per hour

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **35%** - 30% maximum statutory limit, but gratuity principle
**Database Access:** Excellent (official portal well-maintained)
**Note:** Unique system with constitutional gratuity but 30% cost limit

- **Content**: Court of Appeal decisions (2000+), Supreme Court decisions (1999+)
- **Additional**: Jamaica Consolidated Acts, Numbered Acts, Subsidiary Legislation

#### **153. Supreme Court of Jamaica - Judgments**
- **URL**: https://supremecourt.gov.jm/content/judgments
- **Content**: Selected full-text judgments
- **Coverage**: 1996 onwards
- **Access**: **FREE** via official website

#### **154. Court of Appeal Jamaica**
- **URL**: https://www.courtofappeal.gov.jm/
- **Content**: Civil judgments (2002+), Criminal judgments (1994+)
- **Access**: **FREE** via official website

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Stamped Documents**: Fees payable at Stamp Office (not directly to court)
- **Rules of Court**: Prescribe fees for matters filed at Civil Registry
- **"Costs" Definition**: Court fees + attorney's fees + expenses
- **Attorney Fees Cover**: Client interviews, correspondence, document preparation, court appearances

**Fee Increase:**
- Court fees increased by 10% from May 1, 2024

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **30%** - Limited online fee jurisprudence
**Database Access:** Good (multiple free databases, 1994+ coverage)
**Note:** No dedicated JamaicaLII; cases available via CommonLII

### TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO

#### **155. Trinidad and Tobago Judiciary**
- **URL**: https://www.ttlawcourts.org/
- **Law Library**: Practice directions and guides available
- **CourtPay**: Electronic payment system for lawyer fees
- **Access**: Official website

**Practice Guide:**
- Supreme Court Practice Guide to the Assessment of Costs (Gazette No. 113/2015)

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Legal Profession Act**: Governs attorney fees
- **Non-Contentious Business Rules**: Made pursuant to the Act
- **Practice Direction**: Award of Costs issued by Supreme Court
- **Prohibition**: Attorneys cannot charge unfair or unreasonable fees

**Specific Fee Areas:**
- **Probate/Estate**: Scale fees not updated since 1997 (out of sync with inflation)
- **Real Estate**: Based on Stamp Duty Act (Ch. 76:01) + Legal Profession Act (Ch. 90:03)

**Government Expenditure:**
- 2024 Fiscal Year: $158 million paid to private attorneys
- Significant government legal fee expenditure

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **25%** - Statutory framework but outdated scales
**Database Access:** Limited information on case law database
**Note:** No specific "TriniLII" database identified in recent searches
