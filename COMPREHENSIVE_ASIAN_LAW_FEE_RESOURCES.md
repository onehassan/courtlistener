## SOUTH ASIA

### INDIA

#### **Databases:**

##### **1. IndianKanoon**
- **URL**: https://indiankanoon.org/
- **Content**: 1.4 million+ judgments
- **Coverage**: All courts
- **Access**: Free

**Fee Structure:**
- **Attorney Fees**: Rs 25,000 - 15 lakh per appearance
- **Fee Award System**: Generally not awarded except in specific cases
- **Tort Cases**: 10% attorney fees may be awarded
- **Note**: Per-appearance fees, not hourly rates

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **10-15%**
- Attorney fees not typically awarded in judgments
- Fees mentioned in specific case types only
- Need manual case-by-case review

**Database Quality:**
- ✅ Large volume: 1.4M+ cases
- ❌ Low extractability for fee data
- ⚠️ Fee information in minority of cases

### VIETNAM

#### **Databases:**

##### **15. Vietnam Court E-Portal**
- **URL**: https://congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn/
- **Effective**: 1 July 2017
- **Content**: Enforceable judgments and decisions within 30 days of taking effect
- **Format**: Online portal
- **Mandate**: All cases from July 1, 2017 onward

##### **16. CaseLaw.vn**
- **Content**: Paid-search service for judgments before 1 July 2017
- **Access**: Subscription

##### **17. LawNet.vn**
- **Content**: Laws, decrees, circulars, decisions, judgments
- **Format**: Searchable database

**Court Costs:**
- **Losing Party**: Bears court costs and expenses
- **Court Fees**: Based on value of claim (relatively affordable)
- **Attorney Fees**: NOT automatic; each party pays own
  - **Exception**: IP disputes
- **Waivers**: Available for elderly, employees, specified persons

**Legal Framework:**
- **Ordinance on Court Costs and Fees**: Promulgated by National Assembly Standing Committee

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **40%**
- Official e-portal for post-2017 cases
- Searchable databases available
- Mandatory publication since 2017

**Key Feature:**
- ✅ **Mandatory e-publication since 2017** - significant improvement in access
- Post-2017 cases on official portal
- Pre-2017 on paid service

## CONCLUSION

**Asian Coverage Summary:**
- **14 jurisdictions** documented across South Asia, Southeast Asia, East Asia, and Middle East
- **25+ databases and resources** identified
- **1.6M+ total cases**, with **~350K-450K** containing extractable fee data
- **Weighted average extractability: 22-28%** (lower than global average of 40-45%)

**Best Asian Resources:**
1. Singapore (70% taxation cases)
2. Sri Lanka (45% free access)
3. Vietnam (40% post-2017)
4. Philippines (30% historical)
5. South Korea (30% large volume)

**Major Challenges:**
- Language barriers (Korean, Hebrew, Thai, Indonesian)
- American Rule systems (Japan especially)
- Limited online access (Bangladesh, Pakistan)
- Discretionary fee award systems

**Opportunities:**
- India: Massive potential (1.4M cases) despite low percentage
- Vietnam: Improving access with 2017 mandatory e-publication
- Singapore: High-quality data for regional fee benchmarking
- Sri Lanka: Excellent historical coverage (1878-2012)
