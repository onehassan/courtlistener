## How to Search Each Case for Fee Petitions

### Step 1: Open CourtListener URL
Click the URL for the case you want to investigate

### Step 2: Search the Docket
Use browser search (Ctrl+F) to find:
- "attorney fees"
- "costs"
- "fee petition"
- "285" (statute for exceptional case fees)
- "lodestar"
- "prevailing party"

### Step 3: Identify Fee Documents
Look for docket entries like:
- "Motion for Attorney Fees and Costs"
- "Memorandum in Support of Fee Application"
- "Declaration of [Attorney] in Support of Fees"
- "Bill of Costs"
- "Motion for Exceptional Case Fees Under 35 U.S.C. § 285"

### Step 4: Download PDFs
- Click document numbers to download
- Search within PDFs for:
  - "lodestar" (calculation tables)
  - "hourly rate" (attorney billing)
  - "expert witness" (technical consultant fees)
  - Exhibits often contain detailed billing breakdowns

### Step 5: Extract Billing Data
Look for:
- Attorney rates by level (partner, associate, paralegal)
- Expert witness hourly rates
- Technical consultant billing
- Hours expended by task
- Total fees requested
