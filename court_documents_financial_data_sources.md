# Online Sources for Downloadable Court Documents with Financial Data
## Attorney Fees, Hourly Rates, Expert Fees, and Cost Awards

### Research Date: 2025-11-11

---

## UNITED KINGDOM

### 1. **BAILII (British and Irish Legal Information Institute)**
- **URL**: https://www.bailii.org/
- **Content**: Free access to UK court judgments including costs decisions
- **Specific Section**: https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/Costs/
- **Format**: HTML, PDF, RTF downloadable
- **Coverage**: Comprehensive database of England and Wales High Court costs decisions
- **Financial Data**: Detailed assessment judgments, hourly rates, costs awards
- **Example**: PLK & Ors (Court of Protection: Costs) [2020] EWHC B28 (Costs)

### 2. **Find Case Law (National Archives)**
- **URL**: https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/
- **Content**: Judgments and decisions from UK courts and tribunals
- **Format**: Searchable full text, downloadable
- **Coverage**: 2003 onwards
- **Financial Data**: Search for "costs" or "detailed assessment" to find relevant cases

### 3. **Costs Law Reports** (Subscription)
- **URL**: https://www.costslawreports.co.uk/
- **Content**: Largest comprehensive archive of Costs Law cases (civil and criminal)
- **Coverage**: Over 2,300 cases dating back to 1910
- **Financial Data**: Specialized in costs awards, attorney fees, expert fees
- **Note**: Subscription service

### 4. **Senior Courts Costs Office (SCCO) Guide**
- **URL**: https://www.bedsonandchard.com/materials/supervisor/SCCO_Guide_October_2013.pdf
- **Content**: Detailed guidance on costs assessment
- **Format**: PDF downloadable
- **Financial Data**: Hourly rates guidance, assessment procedures

### 5. **Judiciary Guide to Summary Assessment of Costs**
- **URL**: https://www.judiciary.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Guide-to-the-Summary-Assessment-of-Costs-2021-Final1.pdf
- **Format**: PDF downloadable
- **Financial Data**: Costs assessment guidelines and rates

## EUROPE (NON-EU)

### NORWAY

#### 6. **Lovdata**
- **URL**: https://lovdata.no/
- **English Information**: https://lovdata.no/info/information_in_english
- **Content**: Supreme Court and Court of Appeal judgments
- **Format**: Online access (Norwegian language)
- **Coverage**: Free access to judgments from 2008+ (Supreme/Appeal), 2016+ (District)
- **English Resources**: Dispute Act Chapter 20 on legal costs available in English
- **Limitation**: Most judgments only in Norwegian; older than 3 months require subscription

#### 7. **Norwegian Courts (Norges Domstoler)**
- **URL**: https://www.domstol.no/en/
- **English Content**: https://www.domstol.no/en/civil-case/costs/
- **Format**: Information pages and legislation in English
- **Financial Data**: Information on legal costs structure

### SWITZERLAND

#### 8. **Swiss Federal Supreme Court (Bundesgericht)**
- **URL**: https://www.bger.ch/
- **Judgment Database**: https://www.bger.ch/index/juridiction/jurisdiction-inherit-template/jurisdiction-recht.htm
- **Content**: All Federal Supreme Court decisions since 2007
- **Format**: HTML online (no direct PDF download from official site)
- **Coverage**: 127,477 cases (2007-2024)
- **Financial Data**: Search for costs decisions (German, French, Italian)
- **Limitation**: Judgments primarily in German/French/Italian

#### 9. **Swiss Federal Supreme Court Dataset (SCD)**
- **URL**: https://zenodo.org/records/14867950
- **Alternative**: https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/handle/20.500.11850/616000
- **Content**: Complete dataset of all cases 2007-2024
- **Format**: CSV (case metadata) + Parquet (full judgment texts)
- **Coverage**: 127,477 cases with 31 variables including costs information
- **Financial Data**: Downloadable structured data on case outcomes and costs

#### 10. **Federal Administrative Court**
- **URL**: https://www.bvger.ch/en
- **Judgments Database**: https://www.bvger.ch/en/jurisprudence/judgments-database
- **Content**: Administrative court decisions
- **Format**: Online searchable database

### ICELAND

#### 11. **Supreme Court of Iceland (Hæstiréttur)**
- **URL**: https://www.haestirettur.is/en/
- **English Summaries**: https://island.is/en/o/the-supreme-court-of-iceland/legal-summary-of-selected-cases
- **Content**: All judgments published free of charge
- **Format**: Icelandic (full judgments), English (selected case summaries as PDFs)
- **Coverage**: Current judgments available immediately upon pronouncement
- **Limitation**: Full judgments only in Icelandic; English summaries for selected important cases only

## ASIA (EXCLUDING CHINA)

### JAPAN

#### 12. **Supreme Court of Japan**
- **URL**: https://www.courts.go.jp/english/
- **Judgment Search**: https://www.courts.go.jp/app/hanrei_en/search
- **Content**: Selected Supreme Court judgments in English
- **Format**: Online searchable, unofficial translations
- **Financial Data**: Limited - Japanese courts generally don't award attorney fees to winning party
- **Note**: Court costs (filing fees, witness expenses) covered but not attorney fees

#### 13. **Act on the Costs of Civil Proceedings**
- **URL**: https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/3678/en
- **Content**: Full statutory framework for court costs in English
- **Format**: Online text, downloadable

### INDIA

#### 14. **Indian Kanoon**
- **URL**: https://indiankanoon.org/
- **Supreme Court**: https://indiankanoon.org/browse/supremecourt/
- **Content**: Over 1.4 million judgments from Supreme Court and 24 High Courts
- **Format**: HTML, searchable and downloadable
- **Coverage**: Free access to all judgments
- **Search**: https://indiankanoon.org/search/?formInput=advocate+fees
- **Financial Data**: Search for "advocate fees," "costs," "attorney fees"

#### 15. **Law Commission Report on Costs**
- **URL**: https://indiankanoon.org/doc/185277830/
- **Content**: Law Commission of India Report No. 240 on "Costs in Civil Litigation" (2012)
- **Format**: Full text available on IndianKanoon
- **Financial Data**: Comprehensive analysis of costs awards in Indian litigation

#### 16. **Department of Justice Judgment Portal**
- **URL**: https://doj.gov.in/judgment-search-portal/
- **Content**: Official government judgment search
- **Format**: Searchable database

### SINGAPORE

## SEARCH STRATEGIES FOR FINANCIAL DATA

### Recommended Keywords for Searching:
- "costs award" / "costs order"
- "attorney fees" / "attorneys' fees" / "advocate fees"
- "expert fees" / "expert witness fees"
- "hourly rate" / "hourly rates"
- "detailed assessment" / "summary assessment" (UK)
- "taxation of costs" (Commonwealth jurisdictions)
- "bill of costs"
- "legal costs"
- "party and party costs"
- "solicitor and client costs"

### Tips for Finding Financial Data:
1. **UK**: Focus on BAILII Costs section and search for "detailed assessment" cases
2. **Commonwealth Countries**: Search "taxation of costs" in SAFLII, GhaLII, Kenya Law, Singapore ELITIGATION
3. **India**: Use IndianKanoon with keywords "advocate fees" or "Law Commission Report 240"
4. **Civil Law Countries** (Switzerland, Iceland, Norway): Search in local languages for better results
5. **Japan**: Limited attorney fee data due to legal system (each party pays own fees)

## NOTES AND LIMITATIONS

### Language Barriers:
- **Norway**: Most judgments only in Norwegian
- **Switzerland**: German, French, Italian (limited English)
- **Iceland**: Full judgments in Icelandic (English summaries only for selected cases)
- **Japan**: Limited English translations
- **South Korea**: Primarily Korean language

### Access Restrictions:
- **Norway Lovdata**: Older than 3 months requires subscription
- **UK Costs Law Reports**: Subscription required
- **eLaw.my (Malaysia)**: Commercial subscription service

### Attorney Fees Cultural Differences:
- **Japan**: Courts generally don't award attorney fees to winning party (only 10% in tort cases)
- **USA Model**: Rare in Asian jurisdictions except for contractual provisions
- **English Rule**: Common in UK, Commonwealth countries (loser pays)

### Best Free Resources by Region:
- **UK**: BAILII, National Archives Find Case Law
- **Europe**: Swiss Federal Supreme Court Dataset (structured data)
- **Asia**: IndianKanoon (India), ELITIGATION (Singapore), Kenya Law
- **Africa**: SAFLII (South Africa), Kenya Law, GhaLII (Ghana)
