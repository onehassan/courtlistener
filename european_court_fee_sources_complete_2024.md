### SPAIN

#### **136. CENDOJ (Centro de Documentación Judicial)**
- **URL**: https://www.poderjudicial.es (via poderjudicial.es)
- **Content**: Over 9 million judicial rulings
- **Courts Covered**: National High Court, High Courts of Justice, Provincial Courts
- **Access**: **FREE** to all citizens universally
- **Format**: Online searchable database

**Database Features:**
- Comprehensive coverage of all Spanish Collegiate Courts
- Published continuously and freely accessible

#### **137. Portal de Datos de Justicia**
- **URL**: https://datos.justicia.es/en/home
- **Content**: Justice data portal
- **Access**: Public

**Court Fees Structure:**
- **Loser Pays Rule**: Unsuccessful party bears litigation costs
- **Legal Entities**: Court fees applicable to companies and organizations
- **Coverage**: Costs include lawyer fees, court attorney fees, and other party's costs

**Legal Fees:**
- Variable based on case complexity and value
- Final judgment determines litigation cost allocation

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **50%** - Large database, loser pays system
**Note:** 9M+ judgments but cost data requires case-by-case review
**Language:** Spanish (primary), some English resources

- **Content**: Laws, decrees, normative acts, cases
- **Access**: Free government portal

**Court Fees (2024):**

- **Calculation**: Based on dispute amount and proceeding stage
- **First Instance vs. Appeal vs. Supreme Court**: Different rates

### HUNGARY

#### **159. Constitutional Court of Hungary**
- **URL**: https://hunconcourt.hu/
- **Content**: Constitutional Court decisions
- **Access**: Available online

**Major 2024-2025 Court Fee Reforms:**

**Court Fees (Effective January 28, 2025):**
- **Previous System**: 6% of lawsuit value (min HUF 15,000, max HUF 1.5M)
- **New System**: Banded system with NO maximum cap
  - Small cases: Slight reductions
  - Large cases: Significant increases
  - Example: HUF 100M case = ~HUF 2.8M (was HUF 1.5M)
- **Appeal**: Capped at HUF 2.5M
- **Review**: Capped at HUF 3.5M

**Attorney Fees (Effective February 8, 2025):**
- **New Minimum**: HUF 35,000 (5× public defender fee of HUF 7,000)
- **Previous Rate**: ~3× lower
- **Reduction Authority**: Courts may only reduce if party objects AND proves fees unnecessary/disproportionate
- **Maximum Reduction**: 50% (unless grossly excessive)
- **Reasoning Required**: Detailed court reasoning mandatory

**2024 Supreme Court Precedent:**
- Emphasized limits on courts' authority to reduce fees
- Addressed poor practice of arbitrary fee reductions

**Extractability Score:** ⚠️ **40%** - Major 2025 reforms, more predictable fees
**Key Development:** Massive fee structure overhaul for 2025
