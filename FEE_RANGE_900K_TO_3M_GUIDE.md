## How to Find Fee Amounts in Dockets

### Step-by-Step Process:

1. **Open CourtListener URL** for the case
2. **Use Browser Search (Ctrl+F)** to find:
   - "attorney fees"
   - "fee petition"
   - "285" (exceptional case statute)
   - "lodestar"
   - "costs"

3. **Look for These Document Types:**
   - "Motion for Attorney Fees"
   - "Memorandum in Support of Fee Petition"
   - "Declaration in Support of Attorney Fees"
   - "Order Granting/Denying Attorney Fees"
   - "Bill of Costs"

4. **Download PDFs** - Click the document number/link

5. **Search Within PDF** for:
   - Dollar amounts: "$" + "million" or specific amounts
   - "Total fees requested:"
   - "Lodestar amount:"
   - "Fees awarded:"
   - Tables with billing summaries

6. **Check Exhibits:**
   - Exhibit A often contains lodestar calculation tables
   - Look for detailed billing breakdowns
   - Expert witness declarations with rates

## Why Cases in $900K-$3M Range Are Hard to Find

### Factors:

1. **Less Media Coverage:** Smaller fee awards don't generate headlines
2. **Settlement Confidentiality:** Many moderate cases settle with confidential terms
3. **Partial Awards:** Courts often reduce large requests, but orders aren't always published
4. **Database Indexing:** Fee amounts aren't typically indexed/searchable
5. **Sealed Documents:** Some fee petitions (especially with billing details) are sealed

### Where These Cases Hide:

- **Settled Cases:** Fee awards part of confidential settlement
- **Reduced Awards:** $5M request reduced to $2M (court order may not be prominently indexed)
- **Smaller NPEs:** Lesser-known plaintiffs get less coverage
- **Preliminary Injunction Cases:** Fees for interim relief
- **Discovery Sanctions:** Rule 37 fees (like Netlist v. Samsung)

### Approach 2: Look for "Reduced" Fee Awards

Cases where courts cut large fee requests often result in awards in your range:

**Indicators:**
- Order states "request reduced" or "fees reduced"
- Plaintiff requests $5-10M, court awards $1-2M
- "Excessive hours" or "unreasonable rates" language

**Where to Find:**
- Court orders on fee petitions
- Federal Circuit opinions reviewing fee awards
- District court memorandum opinions

---

### Approach 3: Discovery Sanctions Cases

Rule 37 discovery sanctions (like Netlist v. Samsung pending motion) often result in moderate fee awards:

**Search Terms:**
- "Rule 37"
- "discovery sanctions"
- "attorney fees" + "spoliation"
- "fees" + "bad faith"

---

### Approach 4: NPE Cases with Defense Wins

When defendants prevail against NPEs and seek fees as "exceptional case":

**Characteristics:**
- NPE loses on summary judgment
- Court finds case "exceptional" under 35 U.S.C. § 285
- Defense fees typically $1-5M for full litigation
- Partial fees (through summary judgment) could be $900K-$3M

**Companies to Check:**
- Acacia Research cases
- Fortress IP portfolio entities (like VLSI)
- Smaller assertion entities
