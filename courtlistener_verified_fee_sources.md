## How to Find More Cases on CourtListener

### Method 1: Advanced Opinion Search
1. Go to https://www.courtlistener.com/
2. Select "Opinions" search type
3. Use search queries like:
   - `"attorney fees" AND "hourly rate" AND ($ OR dollar)`
   - `"fee petition" AND granted AND "reasonable"`
   - `"lodestar" AND "multiplier"`
4. Filter by:
   - Date range (last 2 years for recent data)
   - Court (federal courts often have more detailed fee orders)
   - Precedential status

### Method 2: RECAP Archive Search
1. Go to https://www.courtlistener.com/recap/
2. Search for documents:
   - `description:"bill of costs"`
   - `description:"fee petition"`
   - `description:"motion for attorney fees"`
3. Filter by:
   - Date filed
   - Available (has PDF)
   - Court

### Method 3: Docket Search
1. Search for specific case types that commonly award fees:
   - Civil rights (42 USC 1983)
   - Employment discrimination (Title VII, ADA)
   - Consumer protection (TCPA, FDCPA)
   - Copyright (17 USC 505)
   - Patent litigation
   - Fee-shifting statutes

### Method 4: Direct URL Pattern
CourtListener RECAP PDFs follow this pattern:
```
https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/[court-code].[case-number]/[court-code].[case-number].[document-number].[attachment-number].pdf
```

Example:
```
https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.ord.178195/gov.uscourts.ord.178195.87.0.pdf
```

## Data Extraction Tips

### From Opinions
**What to Extract:**
- Hourly rates by attorney grade/experience
- Total fees awarded
- Hours worked (if detailed)
- Multipliers applied
- Reduction percentages
- Court's reasoning for fee determination

**Common Opinion Patterns:**
- "The Court awards attorney fees in the amount of $X"
- "Counsel's hourly rate of $X is reasonable"
- "Total hours of X at a rate of $Y"
- "The lodestar amount of $X is multiplied by Y"

### From Fee Petitions (RECAP Documents)
**Document Types to Look For:**
1. **Motion for Attorney Fees** - Initial request
2. **Declaration/Affidavit** - Attorney's statement of work
3. **Billing Records** - Detailed time entries (sometimes filed)
4. **Opposition** - Challenges to fee request
5. **Reply** - Response to opposition
6. **Order** - Court's decision

**What These Contain:**
- Detailed billing statements
- Hourly rates by attorney
- Task descriptions
- Case complexity factors
- Comparable case fee awards
- Local market rate evidence

### Specific Document Entry Keywords
Search docket entries for:
- "Bill of Costs"
- "Motion for Attorney Fees"
- "Fee Petition"
- "Application for Award of Attorney Fees"
- "Taxation of Costs"
- "EAJA Application" (Equal Access to Justice Act)
- "Fee Agreement" (contingency or hourly)
- "Declaration in Support of Attorney Fees"

## Court Types with Best Fee Data

### Federal Courts (Highest Quality)
1. **District Courts** - Most detailed fee petitions
   - Civil rights cases (extensive fee documentation)
   - Employment discrimination
   - Consumer protection
   - Class actions

2. **Bankruptcy Courts** - Fee applications required
   - Trustee fees
   - Attorney fees in bankruptcy cases
   - Fee committees in large cases

3. **Federal Circuit Courts** - Appellate fee awards
   - Often include review of district court fee awards
   - Standards for fee calculation

### State Courts (Variable Quality)
1. **California** - Excellent fee documentation
   - Detailed local standards
   - Prevailing party fee statutes

2. **New York** - Good commercial fee data
   - Commercial Division cases
   - Complex litigation

3. **Delaware Chancery** - Corporate fee awards
   - M&A litigation
   - Shareholder disputes

4. **Texas** - Growing fee documentation
   - Commercial litigation

## Extraction Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: API Authentication Required
**Issue:** Detailed docket information returns 401 Unauthorized
**Solution:**
- Use web scraping for public pages
- Register for API key (free tier available)
- Focus on opinion search (no authentication needed)
- Use RECAP Archive search (publicly accessible)

### Challenge 2: Not All Cases Have Uploaded Documents
**Issue:** Docket shows fee petition filed but no PDF
**Solution:**
- Check multiple document numbers
- Look for fee discussion in main opinion
- Search for case name + "attorney fees" separately
- Contact Free Law Project about purchasing specific documents

### Challenge 3: PDF Format Variability
**Issue:** Fee data in various PDF formats (scanned, native, forms)
**Solution:**
- Use OCR for scanned documents
- Extract structured data from forms
- Parse text-based PDFs with pdfplumber or PyPDF2
- Manual review for complex fee schedules

### Challenge 4: Fee Information Across Multiple Documents
**Issue:** Petition, opposition, reply, order all have different data
**Solution:**
- Download complete docket entry sequence
- Cross-reference amounts across documents
- Prioritize final order for awarded amount
- Use petition for requested amount and hourly rates
