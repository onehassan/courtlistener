## EUROPE - ADDITIONAL SOURCES

### IRELAND

#### **37. Courts Service of Ireland**
- **URL**: https://www.courts.ie/
- **Judgments**: Not directly searchable online (access via court offices)
- **Costs Information**: https://www.courts.ie/content/costs
- **Schedule of Costs**: https://www.courts.ie/content/schedule-costs
- **Content**: Requirements for bills of costs, including grade and qualifications of solicitors
- **Regulations**: S.I. No. 17 of 2014 (District Court Solicitors' Costs Rules), amended by S.I. No. 123 of 2016
- **Format**: PDF documents downloadable for fee schedules and regulations

**Financial Data Available:**
- Bills of costs must show grade of each solicitor/staff member and qualifications
- Where multiple counsel or experts retained, fees must be set out separately
- Office of Legal Costs Adjudicators handles cost adjudication
- General rule: "costs follow the event" (losing party pays)

---

### SCOTLAND

#### **38. Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service**
- **URL**: https://www.scotcourts.gov.uk/
- **Content**: Guidance on expenses (Scottish term for costs)
- **Fee Guide**: https://www.scotcourts.gov.uk/taking-action/court-fees/guide-to-court-fees/
- **Format**: Downloadable forms and guidance documents

**Specific Rates Available:**
- **Unit-Based System (2019+)**: £16.40 per 6-minute unit
- **Effective Date**: Act of Sederunt (Taxation of Judicial Expenses) Rules 2019 (29 April 2019)
- **Courts Covered**: Sheriff Court, Sheriff Appeal Court, Court of Session
- **Capped Expenses**: No award for claims ≤£300; max £150 for claims £300-£1,500

**Access to Judgments:**
- Pro forma expenses claim forms downloadable from SCTS website
- Unit-based system replaced previous block fee system
- Applies to actions raised after 29 April 2019

---

### CYPRUS

#### **39. Cyprus Supreme Court**
- **URL**: Check Supreme Court website (referenced but not directly provided)
- **Bar Association**: https://www.cyprusbarassociation.org/
- **Content**: Court fees and lawyer charges, minimum fees regulations
- **Document**: https://cdn.cyprus-property-buyers.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Cyprus-Bar-Association-Minimum-Fees-Regulations-.pdf

**Financial Data Available:**
- Fees set based on Supreme Court-approved scale of court costs
- Minimum legal fee scales for out-of-court services
- Advocates can agree higher fees with clients above minimum
- Costs awarded to winning party, decided by Court registrar per Bar Association guidelines
- Statistics available on Supreme Court website (Greek and English)

## ASIA-PACIFIC - ADDITIONAL SOURCES

### AUSTRALIA

#### **40. AustLII (Australasian Legal Information Institute)**
- **URL**: http://www.austlii.edu.au/
- **Content**: Court judgments from all Australian courts
- **Coverage**: Comprehensive case law database
- **Format**: HTML, searchable full text

**Financial Data - Federal Court Scale:**
- **Hourly Rate**: Up to **$780 per hour** ($78 per 6-minute unit)
- **Effective**: Work done from 2 September 2025
- **Requirement**: Rates must not exceed rates actually charged to client
- **Bills of Costs**: Must set out hourly rate actually charged

**Typical Market Rates:**
- Graduate lawyers (1-3 years): **$200-400/hour**
- Mid-level lawyers (4-7 years): **$350-550/hour**
- Senior lawyers/partners (8+ years): **$500-800+/hour**

**Barrister Fees (Daily Rates):**
- Junior barristers: **$2,000-5,000/day**
- Senior barristers: **$5,000-10,000/day**
- Queen's Counsel/Senior Counsel: **$8,000-14,000+/day**

## MIDDLE EAST

### UNITED ARAB EMIRATES (UAE)

#### **56. DIFC Courts (Dubai International Financial Centre)**
- **URL**: https://www.difccourts.ae/
- **Fees**: https://www.difccourts.ae/about/fees
- **BAILII Access**: https://www.bailii.org/recent-decisions-ae.html
- **Content**: Recent DIFC court judgments (20 most recent in reverse chronological order)
- **Format**: Free downloadable soft copies via eRegistry

**Specific Court Fees (USD):**

**Filing Fees:**
- Non-monetary claims: **$5,000**
- Monetary claims: Varies by Part 7 RDC

**Enforcement Fees:**
- DIFC judgments/orders: **$300**
- Outside DIFC judgments: **1% of value** (min $100, max $20,000)

**Application Fees:**
- Without hearing: **$300**
- Oral hearing (≤2 hours): **$1,000**

**Legal Costs Recovery:**
- DIFC Court can issue interim costs orders
- Usually 50% of incurred costs or minimum expected recovery
- Assessment on standard or indemnity basis

**Comparison - UAE Onshore Courts:**
- NO procedure for legal fees recovery
- Successful party recovers only court fees and expert fees
- Only token sum for legal costs

### INVESTMENT ARBITRATION

#### **65. ICSID (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes)**
- **URL**: https://icsid.worldbank.org/
- **Cost Information**: https://icsid.worldbank.org/services/cost-of-proceedings
- **Schedule of Fees 2023**: https://icsid.worldbank.org/services/cost-of-proceedings/schedule-fees/2023
- **Schedule of Fees 2020**: https://icsid.worldbank.org/services/cost-of-proceedings/schedule-fees

**Specific Costs:**
- **Administrative Charge**: **US$52,000** upon registration, then annually
- **Arbitrator Fees**: **US$3,000 per day** of meetings or work
- **Average Claimant Costs (FY2011-2015)**: **US$5,619,261.74** (55 cases where data available)

**Cost Allocation:**
- Tribunal has broad discretion to allocate costs in final award
- Parties responsible for own legal representation and expert fees
- Cost data publicly available for 55 claimant cases and 56 respondent cases (FY2011-2015)
- Discontinued/settled proceedings: Cost data not publicly available

**Data Sources:**
- Jus Mundi database: Sample of publicly available arbitral awards

#### **66. PCA (Permanent Court of Arbitration)**
- **URL**: https://pca-cpa.org/
- **Fees & Costs**: https://pca-cpa.org/fees-and-costs/
- **Costs Information**: https://pca-cpa.org/costs/
- **PCA Arbitration Rules 2012**: https://docs.pca-cpa.org/2015/11/PCA-Arbitration-Rules-2012.pdf

- **Tribunal/Institutional Costs**: 10-15% of total arbitration costs

- Control over deposit requests
- Automatic review of total arbitrator fees at end of proceedings

**Downloadable Resources:**
- Final Awards Regarding Costs available on pcacases.com

- Electronic access via www.kluwerarbitration.com

### TRADE DISPUTES

#### **67. WTO Dispute Settlement**
- **URL**: https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/dispu_e.htm
- **Legal Text**: https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/dispu_e/dsu_e.htm

**Legal Fees Context:**
- Private law firms costly, especially for developing countries
- Specialized WTO lawyers primarily in developed countries' capitals (Washington, Brussels, Geneva, Paris, London)
- Private firms/trade associations may underwrite legal fees for government complaints
