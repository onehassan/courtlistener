## Data Points to Extract from Each Case

For each case above, review the docket for documents containing:

### 1. **Matter Scope**
- Patent claims at issue
- Technology area
- Accused products/services
- Number of patents asserted

### 2. **Case Phases**
- Claim construction completed?
- Discovery phase status
- Summary judgment?
- Trial status
- Appeal?

### 3. **Attorney Fees & Billing Data**
Look for documents titled:
- "Motion for Attorney Fees"
- "Fee Petition"
- "Application for Costs and Fees"
- "Declaration in Support of Fees"
- "Billing Records" (often as exhibits)
- Fee awards in final orders

### 4. **Rate Information**
- Partner hourly rates
- Associate hourly rates
- Paralegal rates
- Blended rates
- Lodestar calculations
- Rate enhancements/multipliers

### 5. **Parties & Counsel**
- Law firms representing each party
- Lead counsel names
- Firm size/tier (AmLaw 100, boutique, etc.)
- In-house counsel involvement

### 6. **Outcomes**
- Who prevailed?
- Fee-shifting statutory basis (35 U.S.C. § 285 for patent cases)
- Fee amounts awarded
- Fee amounts requested vs. granted

## Notes on Data Collection

1. **Fee Disclosure Quality**: Look for cases with:
   - Detailed billing records as exhibits
   - Lodestar calculations with hours breakdown
   - Timekeeper descriptions (partner/associate/paralegal)
   - Hourly rate justifications

2. **Comparable Case Selection**: The Patent Armory cases are particularly valuable because they:
