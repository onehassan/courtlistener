## Fee Structure Patterns: Global Analysis

### Hourly Rate Systems
**Countries:** UK, US, Canada, Australia, Norway, Netherlands

**Typical Ranges:**
- Junior lawyers: $150-300/hour (USD equivalent)
- Mid-level: $250-500/hour
- Senior/Partners: $400-1,000+/hour
- Specialists: Can exceed $1,500/hour

### Statutory Fee Systems
**Countries:** Germany, Austria, Scotland, South Korea

**Characteristics:**
- Fixed calculation formulas
- Based on case value or complexity
- Predictable and transparent
- Often includes published tables

### Hybrid Systems
**Countries:** France, Poland, Thailand, Indonesia

**Characteristics:**
- Combination of fixed and variable fees
- Court-ordered costs + market rates
- Often caps or minimums
- Percentage-based alternatives

## Data Standardization Framework

### Core Fields to Extract
**Essential:**
- Jurisdiction
- Court level
- Case citation
- Decision date
- Party names (plaintiff/defendant or applicant/respondent)
- Case type/category
- Fee amount (local currency)
- Fee type (hourly rate, daily rate, total award, filing fee)
- Attorney grade/experience level
- Winning/losing party

**Highly Valuable:**
- Fee breakdown (preparation, hearing, research, etc.)
- Time spent (hours/days)
- Complexity factors
- Geographic location (if sub-national rates)
- Practice area
- Court reasoning for fee award

**Nice to Have:**
- Full text of judgment
- Dissenting opinions on costs
- Appeal history
- Settlement vs. judgment
- Pro bono indicators

### Currency Normalization
**Approach:**
- Maintain original currency
- Add USD equivalent field
- Include exchange rate date
- Historical rate tracking for trend analysis
- PPP adjustments for international comparisons

### Date Standardization
**Format:** ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD)

**Track:**
- Filing date
- Decision date
- Fee award date (if different)
- Publication date

## Risk Assessment

### Legal Risks
**Terms of Service Violations:**
- **Mitigation:** Review ToS for each source
- **Approach:** Respect robots.txt, rate limiting
- **Strategy:** Prioritize explicitly free/open sources

**Copyright Issues:**
- **Mitigation:** Extract facts only (not copyrightable)
- **Approach:** Transform data substantially
- **Strategy:** Cite sources appropriately

### Technical Risks
**Source Changes:**
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium to High
- **Mitigation:** Monitoring system, flexible scrapers

**Data Quality Degradation:**
- **Probability:** Low to Medium
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Mitigation:** Automated quality checks, validation

### Operational Risks
**Resource Requirements:**
- **Storage:** Moderate (estimated 100GB-1TB for full dataset)
- **Processing:** Significant (multi-language NLP, PDF extraction)
- **Maintenance:** Ongoing (source monitoring, updates)

**Team Expertise:**
- Required: Legal research, web scraping, NLP, database management
- Nice to have: Multi-language capabilities, legal domain knowledge
